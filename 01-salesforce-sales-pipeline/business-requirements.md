# Business Requirements Document

## Project: Salesforce Sales Pipeline Management

### Business Scenario
A fictional B2B sales company needs to improve opportunity tracking, pipeline visibility, and reporting accuracy.

### Stakeholders (Simulated)
- Sales Manager: Needs accurate pipeline forecasts and performance reporting.
- Sales Representatives: Need a consistent process for updating opportunities.
- Sales Operations Analyst: Needs reliable CRM data and streamlined reporting.
- Salesforce Administrator: Responsible for configuring and maintaining the solution.

### Functional Requirements
- BR-001: Sales representatives must be able to create and update opportunities.
- BR-002: Opportunities must include an account, stage, close date, and opportunity name.
- BR-003: Users must select a loss reason when an opportunity is marked Closed Lost.
- BR-004: Sales managers must be able to view pipeline reports by stage and owner.
- BR-005: Sales managers must be able to view expected revenue and upcoming close dates.

### Nonfunctional Requirements
- CRM data must be accessible only to authorized users.
- Reports should be understandable and easy to maintain.
- The solution should use standard Salesforce features where practical.

### Acceptance Criteria (Proposed)
- Opportunities can be created with the required fields.
- An opportunity cannot be saved as Closed Lost without a loss reason.
- Reports correctly summarize sample opportunities by stage and owner.
- Users with appropriate access can view the dashboard.

### Project Status
Requirements drafted for a simulated case study. Stakeholder review, configuration, and testing are pending.

*All business scenarios, stakeholders, and data are fictional.*
